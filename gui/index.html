About

MeteoGlance

Live weather observations, around the world.

MeteoGlance maps measurements reported by APRS and CWOP weather stations worldwide. Explore observed temperature, wind speed and gusts, rainfall, humidity, and atmospheric pressure.

Explore the map

Choose a weather icon to change the measurement shown on the map. Select a station to see its latest readings and observation time. A plus badge marks a group of stations; its number is their average. Zoom in to explore individual stations.

See how the weather changes

Open History in a station’s details for day, week, month, and year charts. These show available observations, with averages and peaks appropriate to each measurement. Gaps mean no readings were received.

Where the data comes from

Readings come from the Automatic Packet Reporting System (APRS) and the Citizen Weather Observer Program (CWOP). These are station observations, rather than forecasts. Availability and accuracy depend on the reporting station.

Use the location button to center the map near you, and °C / °F to switch between metric and imperial units. Rain charts show the reported rolling 24-hour rainfall, not a total for the selected period.
